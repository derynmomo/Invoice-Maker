Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ledger ("the app") lets you create invoices by speaking your work details. This policy explains what data the app handles. You must host this page at a public URL and link it from your App Store Connect and Google Play listings.

1. What we collect

The app uses your device microphone and speech-recognition services to convert your spoken words into text so it can fill in an invoice. The transcribed text is used only to populate invoice fields (description, date, hours, rate, materials) and is processed on your device or by your device's built-in speech recognition service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android).

Invoice information you enter — client names, phone numbers, email addresses, service descriptions and amounts — is stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and no cloud storage.

2. How we use the data

  • To generate invoice PDFs you choose to download.
  • To prepare invoice text you choose to share via SMS/Messages or other apps.
  • To improve voice parsing accuracy for your own use.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. When you share an invoice, the data goes through the sharing mechanism you choose (your messaging app, email, etc.).

4. Permissions

The app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. You can deny or revoke these at any time in your device settings.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app means you accept the current version.

7. Contact

Questions? Email the address listed in your app store listing.
